# Code of conduct

Everyone contributing to this registry, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is
expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected

- Be kind and precise. Review the science and the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before judging.
- Say what was tested and what was not. Honest declarations are part of the contract here.
- Credit the tools and authors a skill builds on.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information, including patient data, credentials or personal
  paths, in any form.
- Sustained disruption of reviews or discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the registry owners listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS)
by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/lijinbio/aiscientist-skills/security/advisories/new)
or contacting them directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The owners may edit,
remove or reject contributions, and may block contributors, when this code is not followed.
